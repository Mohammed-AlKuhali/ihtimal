# Backtest: honest numbers

Pre-registered in [`BACKTEST_SPEC.md`](BACKTEST_SPEC.md) before any metric was computed. Code: `model/backtest.py`. Generated by `model/write_backtest_md.py` from `data/backtest_eu.json` and `data/backtest_spl.json`; nothing below was typed by hand.

## In one paragraph

On a full season the model never saw (2025-26, refit every week using only earlier matches), it called the match result (home win, draw or away win) correctly in **52.2% of 1,937 European matches** (big-5 leagues plus the Champions League) and **50.2% of 1,065 matches in the Premier League, La Liga and Ligue 1**. Its single most-likely exact score came up in **10.5% of matches (about 1 in 9.5)** and **11.3% (about 1 in 8.9)** in the three site leagues. It is clearly better than base rates, about level with a plain Elo model, and not tested against bookmakers (that data is deliberately not used). In the Saudi league it got 57.5% of 306 matches right in 2025-26 (exact score 8.5%, about 1 in 11.8). Football stays random: a "good" model is right about half the time.

| Elo leg in domestic matches | Not used. The best blend did not beat Dixon-Coles alone by the pre-registered 0.0005 RPS on 2024-25 (best blend: 0.2015 vs Dixon-Coles only: 0.2015 domestic RPS), so w = 1 |
| Elo leg in Champions League matches | w = 0.3 (30% Dixon-Coles, 70% Elo; the blend gave lower RPS on the 2024-25 Champions League matches). **Noisy: only 185 matches.** On the untouched 2025-26 holdout the blend was slightly *worse* than Dixon-Coles only (see below), and the difference is within noise |
| Saudi Pro League model | Separate Dixon-Coles on Saudi results only, xi = 0.012 (weights halve every 58 days; faster decay for summer squad churn), ridge 8, rho estimated with one-year-half-life weights and held fixed (Amendment 1 in BACKTEST_SPEC.md), blended with its own Elo leg at w = 0.3 (30% Dixon-Coles, 70% Elo) |
| Data | openfootball (public domain) plus ESPN public scoreboard JSON. Where both cover a match they agree on all 5,355 overlapping results. Saudi league and Champions League: ESPN only |
| Not used | Bookmaker odds, football-data.co.uk, Football Charts, ClubElo ratings (display only), xG (only in the three featured games' adjustment) |

## What this does and does not show

* **Leakage guards.** Every fitted object (Dixon-Coles parameters, Elo ratings, the Elo mappings, base rates) is rebuilt each week from matches strictly before that week; an assertion checks the dates in every fold. A perturbation test (`python model/backtest.py selfcheck`) scrambles every result inside a test week and confirms the predictions for that week do not change at all.
* **Data quality.** The same match from two independent sources always had the same score; the only gap is that ESPN is the sole source for the Saudi league and the Champions League.
* **One holdout season.** 2025-26 is a single sample. Differences of 0.001-0.003 RPS between the model and Elo-only are inside the bootstrap interval.
* **No market comparison.** Bookmaker odds are not used anywhere (not even privately), so there is no claim about "beating the market". Published bookmaker-based models typically sit a few thousandths of RPS better than Elo-style models.
* **Adjustments are not backtested.** The injury/suspension and xG adjustments on the three featured games are heuristic. The public tally counts the adjusted headline, `base` is shown next to it, both are stored and both are scored.
* **Exact scores.** About 1 in 9 is normal for the most likely score of any model. Treat it as a cheerful bonus, not a promise.
* **Early-season refits.** Between August and October a team has only a few matches of the new season, so the weights lean on last season; promoted clubs start near their league average.

## Head-to-head (Amendment 2)

Question (Mo): does the last-10-meetings record between the two clubs improve the forecast? Pre-registered in `BACKTEST_SPEC.md` (Amendment 2) before any metric with the feature was computed; code `model/h2h_experiment.py`, results `data/h2h_experiment.json`.

**Feature.** For each match, the last up to 10 meetings strictly before the weekly cut-off (leagues, second tiers, European cups, domestic cups; no friendlies). For each, actual goal difference minus the goal difference that week's own Dixon-Coles fit expects, weighted by recency (half-life 3 years), shrunk toward zero with 5 pseudo-meetings: `R`. Expected goals are then multiplied by `exp(+w R)` (home) and `exp(-w R)` (away). `w` in {0.0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2}; `w = 0` reproduces the shipped model exactly (checked against the tuning grid). **Acceptance rule (set in advance):** accept only if 2024-25 RPS improves by at least 0.0005 over `w = 0` with log-loss not higher; the 2025-26 holdout is run once, and only if accepted.
