# site/data: exact schema (as produced, 19 Oct 2026)

All files are UTF-8 JSON, generated by `model/predict_live.py` (+ `add_context.py`, `score.py`, `export_meta.py`). Times are UTC ISO-8601 with `Z`. Probabilities are fractions 0-1 rounded to 6 decimals (show as whole percentages; make sure the three rounded numbers add up to 100). Team names: use `ar` (Arabic) and `en`; **home is always the first team**. KSA time = UTC+3 all year; UK = UTC+1 until 02:00 on Sun 25 Oct 2026, then UTC+0.

```
site/data/
  featured.json              the three featured games (full match objects)
  leagues/spl.json           upcoming fixtures, next 14 days (summaries)
  leagues/epl.json  leagues/laliga.json  leagues/ligue1.json
  matches/<match_id>.json    one full file per match (87 files: 85 league fixtures including the Clasico, plus the 2 Champions League featured games)
  record.json                results vs predictions (all pending now)
  locks.json                 sha256 + UTC lock time of the three featured files
  meta.json                  generation time, sources/credits, coverage, backtest numbers, disclaimer
```

Match ids: `YYYY-MM-DD_<home-id>-<away-id>` (date = UTC kick-off date). Featured: `2026-10-20_psg-barcelona`, `2026-10-21_bayern-arsenal`, `2026-10-25_barcelona-real-madrid`.

### `adjustments[]` (featured games)

Common: `kind` (`xg` / `xg_combined` / `availability`), `team` (team id), `multiplier` (number, applied to a team's expected goals), `applies_to` (`home` / `away` / `own` / `opponent` / `none`), `effect_en`, `effect_ar`, `checked_at`.

* `kind: "xg"`: `type` = `attack` or `defence`, `xg`, `goals`, `games`, `metric`, `source` (statz.ai). Each factor is `sqrt(xG / goals)` clipped to 0.92-1.08.
* `kind: "xg_combined"`: the xG factor actually applied to the home or away side (own attack x opponent defence, clipped again to 0.92-1.08).
* `kind: "availability"`: `player`, `player_ar` (may be null), `position`, `status` (`out`, `suspended`, `doubt`, `knock`), `p_out` (1.0 / 0.5 / 0.2), `regular` (>= 50% of the club's available minutes this season), `minutes_share`, `goal_involvement_share` (attackers only), `type` (`attack`, `defence`, `goalkeeper`, or `none` when not a regular), `note`, `source` (URL). Non-regulars are listed with multiplier 1 and `applies_to: "none"`.

Rules: attacker absent: own expected goals x (1 - 0.4 x share x p_out), floor 0.85. Regular centre-back/full-back absent: opponent x (1 + 0.03 p_out); first-choice goalkeeper: x (1 + 0.04 p_out); defensive boosts summed and capped at 1.08.

## leagues/<key>.json

`{league: {key, ar, en}, generated_at, window_days: 14, window_end_utc, data_cutoff_utc, model_version, n_matches, matches: [...]}` sorted by kick-off. Each match: `match_id, kickoff_utc, venue, home{id,ar,en,colour}, away{...}, p_home, p_draw, p_away, pick, top_scores, lambda_home, lambda_away, featured, has_adjustments, locked, sha256`. Window = kick-off after the generation time (`generated_at`) and up to 14 days later (`window_end_utc`). **Matches whose kick-off has passed by the time the page is viewed should be hidden or shown as played**: three Saudi, one Premier League and one La Liga game kick off later on 19 Oct, so some entries are already past when the site goes live. Per the critic note, show only the W/D/L bars in the league lists and keep the exact-score pick for the match page. Counts: SPL 26, EPL 20, La Liga 21 (includes the Clasico), Ligue 1 18.

## featured.json

`{generated_at, matches: [3 full match objects]}` in order PSG v Barcelona, Bayern v Arsenal, Barcelona v Real Madrid.

## record.json

`{updated_at, model_version, rules, rows[], tally, tracked_summary}`. Each row: `match_id, competition, kickoff_utc, home{id,ar,en}, away{id,ar,en}, featured, locked, counts_in_tally, generated_at, locked_at, sha256, prediction{p_home,p_draw,p_away,pick,headline}, base_prediction{...}, status ("pending"|"final"), result ({home_goals, away_goals, outcome} or null), result_correct, score_correct, base_result_correct, base_score_correct` (null until final). The three locked featured games have `counts_in_tally: true`; the other 84 rows are tracked but not hash-locked and not part of the headline tally. `tally` = `{featured_total, scored, result_correct, score_correct, base_result_correct, base_score_correct}`. Update with `python model/score.py` after full time. Misses stay in `rows`.

## locks.json

`{locked_at_utc, model_version, algorithm, sequence[], matches: [{match_id, file, sha256, locked_at_utc, kickoff_utc, headline{p_home,p_draw,p_away,pick}, base{...}}], relock_history?}`. `relock_history` lists any earlier lock that was replaced before publication, with its hashes and the reason.

**Verify a lock** (`python model/lockutil.py site/data/matches/<id>.json` prints OK or MISMATCH). The hash is sha256 of the canonical JSON of the file with these removed: top-level `sha256`, `locked_at`, `benchmarks`, `sanity`, and `elo.clubelo_home`, `elo.clubelo_away` (those are added after the lock). Canonical form: keys sorted, no whitespace, strings as UTF-8 JSON (no `\u` escapes for non-ASCII), integers as integers, floats as fixed 6 decimals with trailing zeros removed (`0.5`, `1` for 1.0), `true/false/null`. Locked files are immutable; a correction is a new `..._v2.json` that never replaces v1.

## meta.json

`generated_at, data_cutoff_utc, model_version, sources[] {name, use, credit, url, licence?}, not_used[], model{summary_en, european, saudi, grid, headline_pick_rule, adjustments}, data_coverage{<comp>: {name, source, matches_per_season, last_result_date, total_matches, fixtures_predicted_next_14_days?}}, espn_fetched_utc, data_checks, backtest{protocol, europe_holdout_2025_26, site_leagues_holdout_2025_26, champions_league_holdout_2025_26, europe_2026_27_to_date, saudi_holdout_2025_26, saudi_2026_27_to_date}, locks, disclaimer{ar,en}, author`.

Each backtest block: `n, wdl_accuracy, headline_score_hit, one_exact_score_in, rps, rps_base_rates, rps_elo_only, rps_uniform, log_loss, log_loss_base_rates, home_win_always_accuracy, rps_diff_vs_base_rates_ci95 [mean, lo, hi], rps_diff_vs_elo_only_ci95 [mean, lo, hi]` (accuracy and hit rates as fractions). Full tables: `BACKTEST.md` in the project root. Honest line for the method page: right about half the time (52% of 1,937 holdout matches, 50% in the three site leagues), exact score about 1 in 9-10.

## Content rules kept in the data

No betting vocabulary, no market data, no crests or photos in any JSON here. (`BACKTEST.md` and `BACKTEST_SPEC.md` are internal methodology documents and do discuss what is deliberately *not* used; take numbers from them, do not copy their wording onto the public site.) Credits to show: openfootball, ESPN, `Elo: clubelo.com`, `xG: statz.ai`, `Benchmark: Opta / The Analyst`.

## h2h/<match_id>.json (added 19 Oct 2026; one file per match in `matches/`)

Last up to 10 competitive meetings of the two clubs before the kick-off date, most recent first. Built by `model/h2h.py` from ESPN's public scoreboard JSON and openfootball (public domain). **Display only: nothing in it enters the probabilities** (the experiment that tested it was not accepted, see BACKTEST.md "Head-to-head (Amendment 2)").

`{match_id, generated_at, data_through, home{id,en,ar}, away{id,en,ar}, perspective, meetings[], summary, coverage, sources}`

* `meetings[]`: `date`, `competition{key,en,ar}`, `stage` (ESPN slug such as `round-of-16`, `quarterfinals`, `final`, `group-stage`, `league-phase`; null for league matches) and `stage_en`, `home{id,en,ar}`, `away{...}` (who hosted **that day**; a neutral final keeps ESPN's label), `home_goals`, `away_goals`, `decided` (`ft`, `aet` = score includes extra time, `pens` = score after extra time, shoot-out in `penalties{home,away}`), plus the same meeting seen from the fixture's home team: `fixture_home_goals`, `fixture_away_goals`, `fixture_home_team_result` (`W`/`D`/`L`).
* `summary`: `n`, `home_wins`, `draws`, `away_wins`, `home_goals`, `away_goals` (all from the **fixture home team's** side, whichever ground it was played on), `decided_on_penalties_counted_as_draws`.
* `coverage`: `found`, `wanted` (10), `complete`, `covered_since`, `competitions_covered`, and, when `found < 10`, `note_en` / `note_ar` explaining why (show them). Saudi pairs are the main gap: ESPN's Saudi record starts in August 2022 and covers only the top flight (plus King's Cup, AFC Champions League), so no Saudi file has 10 meetings and newly promoted clubs can have 0 (then `meetings` is `[]`).
* Competitions covered: five leagues and their second tiers, Saudi Pro League, Champions / Europa / Conference League, UEFA Super Cup, domestic cups and super cups of England, Spain, France, Germany and Italy, King's Cup, AFC Champions League, Club World Cup. No friendlies.
